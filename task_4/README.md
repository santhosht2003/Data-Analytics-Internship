#  Task 4 – Dashboard Design (Power BI)

##  Objective
The goal of this task is to design an interactive and visually clear dashboard using Power BI.  
The dashboard should help business users understand key sales and profit trends quickly and easily.

---

##  Dataset Used
**Sample Superstore Dataset**  
(Downloaded from Kaggle)

This dataset contains:
- Order Date
- Ship Date
- Sales
- Profit
- Category & Sub-Category
- Region & State
- Customer Information

---

##  Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Sample Superstore CSV file**
- **GitHub** for final submission

---

##  Dashboard Components (Visuals Created)

###  1. KPI Cards
- Total Sales  
- Total Profit  
- Total Orders  
- Average Sales  

###  2. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)
- X-axis: Order Date (Year-Month)
- Y-axis: Sales

###  3. Profit by Category (Bar Chart)
- Shows which category gives the most profit

###  4. Sales by Region (Donut Chart)
- Region-wise contribution to sales

###  8. Slicers (Interactive Filters)
- Year
- Category
- Region

---

##  Dashboard Design Approach
- Clean and minimal design  
- Consistent color theme  
- Simple dropdown slicers  
- Important KPIs placed at top  
- Avoided clutter  
- Easy navigation and readability  

---

##  Key Insights
- Technology category brings the highest sales.
- West region contributes the most profit.
- Sales increase significantly during year-end.
- California is the top-performing state.
- A few products have negative profits.
- Top 10 products contribute a major portion of revenue.
